<h3>Brief Overview</h3>

<p> Before the 2023 Free Agency period opened up, I wanted to use my modeling skills and see if I could predict the values and lengths of contracts that players would end up signing (different from predicting how much they were really worth!). I ended up scraping and using data from <a href="https://www.spotrac.com/nba/free-agents">Spotrac</a> for contracts and <a href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/">Basketball Reference</a> for player stats. Once I created my model and extracted the predictions, I followed up on free agents I found particulary interesting and made some explanations on why my model may have said what it did.</p>
